Our Heritage & Craft

Founded with a passion for theatrical artistry, cultural heritage, and expressive storytelling through handcrafted attire.

Our Purpose

Authentic Outfits for Every Stage & Celebration

COSTUME STORE was created to solve a persistent challenge faced by performers, parents, theatre directors, and cosplayers: the lack of durable, accurately tailored, and brand-new costume wear available for direct purchase in India.

Unlike rental services with worn fabrics and uncertain hygiene, we design and produce brand new, sales-only garments. Every stitch, embroidery lace, and metallic trim is created to be owned, cherished, and worn with pride.

Theatrical Grade Fabrics

We select rich brocades, breathable cotton blends, stretchable 4-way spandex, and authentic zari trims designed to dazzle under stage illumination while ensuring actor comfort.

Precision Fit & Sizing

Every costume features standardized size grading from toddler fancy dress (2-3Y) up to adult XXL, backed by detailed measurement guides and flexible adjustment ties.

Complete Ensembles

No need to source individual accessories separately. Our costume packages include matching stoles, belts, fabric masks, crowns, and props ready straight out of the box.
